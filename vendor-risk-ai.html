Healthcare Compliance Framework

HIPAA Security Rule Requirements

The HIPAA Security Rule establishes national standards to protect electronic protected health information (ePHI). All vendors handling ePHI must demonstrate compliance with:

  • Administrative Safeguards (Risk Analysis, Workforce Training, Contingency Planning)
  • Physical Safeguards (Facility Access, Workstation Security, Device Controls)
  • Technical Safeguards (Access Control, Audit Controls, Transmission Security)
  • Organizational Requirements (Business Associate Agreements)
  • Policies and Procedures Documentation

HITECH Act Breach Notification

The HITECH Act strengthened HIPAA enforcement and introduced breach notification requirements:

  • Breach notification within 60 days of discovery
  • Risk assessment methodology for determining reportable breaches
  • Enhanced penalties ($100 - $50,000 per violation, up to $1.5M annually)
  • Business Associate direct liability for HIPAA violations
  • Mandatory audits and enforcement

Oklahoma-Specific Requirements

Oklahoma healthcare organizations must comply with state-specific regulations:

  • Oklahoma Security Breach Notification Act (HB 2663) - 45-day notification requirement
  • Oklahoma Health Information Exchange (OHIE) security standards
  • Oklahoma Medicaid SoonerCare requirements for vendors
  • State privacy laws for medical records (63 O.S. § 1-502.2)
  • Oklahoma Department of Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services (ODMHSAS) requirements

NIST Cybersecurity Framework

Industry best practice framework for managing cybersecurity risk:

  • Identify: Asset management, risk assessment, governance
  • Protect: Access control, data security, training
  • Detect: Anomaly detection, continuous monitoring
  • Respond: Response planning, incident management
  • Recover: Recovery planning, business continuity

Comprehensive Vendor Risk Assessment Checklist

Based on 20+ years of healthcare security experience in Oklahoma

1. Legal & Compliance (Critical)

  • Business Associate Agreement (BAA) executed and current
  • HIPAA Security Rule compliance attestation
  • Oklahoma breach notification procedures documented
  • Subcontractor/downstream BAA management process
  • Insurance coverage: Cyber liability ($2M+ recommended for high-risk vendors)
  • Legal jurisdiction clauses appropriate for Oklahoma

2. Security Controls & Certifications

  • SOC 2 Type II report (within 12 months)
  • HITRUST CSF certification (preferred for healthcare)
  • ISO 27001 certification
  • Penetration testing results (annual minimum)
  • Vulnerability scanning reports
  • Security incident history and response

3. Data Protection & Encryption

  • Encryption at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+ minimum)
  • Key management procedures
  • Data retention and destruction policies
  • Backup and disaster recovery procedures
  • Data segregation between clients

4. Access Controls & Authentication

  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforcement
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Principle of least privilege implementation
  • User access review procedures
  • Privileged access management
  • Session timeout and termination controls

5. Monitoring & Incident Response

  • 24/7 security monitoring and logging
  • Log retention (minimum 6 years for HIPAA)
  • Incident response plan documented
  • Breach notification procedures (60-day HIPAA, 45-day Oklahoma)
  • Security incident contact information
  • Forensic investigation capabilities

6. Vendor Management & Due Diligence

  • Subcontractor security assessment process
  • Fourth-party risk management
  • Right to audit clause in contract
  • SLA with security metrics and uptime guarantees
  • Exit strategy and data portability
  • Vendor financial stability assessment

7. Operational Security

  • Security awareness training program
  • Background checks for personnel with PHI access
  • Change management procedures
  • Patch management and vulnerability remediation
  • Network segmentation and firewalls
  • Secure software development lifecycle (if applicable)

8. Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

  • Business continuity plan (BCP) documented and tested
  • Disaster recovery plan (DRP) with RTO/RPO defined
  • Redundant systems and failover procedures
  • Annual DR testing results
  • Geographic diversity of data centers
  • Backup restoration verification

Risk Scoring Matrix

MEDIUM (40-69) Moderate risk. Additional controls or monitoring recommended. May approve with conditions.
